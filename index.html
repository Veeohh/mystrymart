Before you apply

You'll need

  • To live and make your art in Florida
  • Work that's 100% original and made by you
  • A cohesive, established style
  • A public, art-focused Instagram. Follower count doesn't matter. We put your handle on the packaging so buyers can find and support you.
  • Social handles and your website on your piece are welcome

We can't accept

  • Discounts or promo codes of any kind
  • Zines, stand-alone stickers or sticker sheets, pins, keychains or greeting cards
  • Controversial, religious or political themes, or holiday content
  • AI-generated work
  • Licensed characters, famous people or celebrities

Good to know

Shipping timeline

Prints ship within 14 days of when we request them. For consignment, your agreement spells out how much time you have to make and send your work.

Packaging is required

If your work arrives without the required packaging listed here and in your agreement, we deduct $50 for our team's materials and time to repackage it.

Changing your work

Want to send something different from what you applied with? Email us first with photos of the new work, and we'll let you know.

Getting work back

You can ask for your work back at any time. You cover the return shipping label.

What we look for

Artists who create out of passion and as a livelihood. People who see their work as a pursuit of happiness, and who have a vision for where it's going.

How it works

  1. Apply

    Fill out the form below. It takes about 10 minutes.

  2. We review

    Our team looks at your work, style and Instagram, and reaches out as spots open up.

  3. Artist agreement

    If it's a good fit, we email you an agreement to sign.

  4. Payment setup

    You get an email from Melio to add your bank details (or choose checks) and fill out a W-9.

  5. Portal login

    You get your artist portal, where you track orders, send tracking and see payouts.

  6. You go live

    We add your page to our site, request your pieces, package them and start selling.

Questions?

Buying art

What is Mystry Mart?

An art vending machine. We turn snack machines into mini galleries stocked with sealed bags of original work by independent artists.

What's inside a bag?

One original piece, like a fine art print, a painting, a ceramic or another unique oddity. What you get is the mystery.

Can I choose what I get?

Sort of. Each bag shows the artist's Instagram handle and a QR code, so you can pick by artist. Or choose at random and let it surprise you.

How much does it cost?

At our machines, artists set their own prices, usually between $10 and $100. Every bag in our online machine is $25.

How do I pay at a machine?

Our machines are cashless. Just tap or swipe your card, or tap your phone. If tap doesn't work, please swipe instead.

My item got stuck. What do I do?

Sorry to hear that! Let a staff member at the venue know, or take a photo of the stuck item and send it to us through our contact form with the last 4 digits of the card you used. Once we look into what happened, we'll give you a full refund.

What if I don't like my item?

Gift it! We can't offer refunds for items you don't like, which is why we encourage you to scan the QR code on each bag first to see the artist's style. Still not your thing? Pass it on to someone who'll love it. Either way, you've supported a real, living artist, and that goes a long way.

How does buying online work?

Pick a bag in the online machine, hit vend and push the flap to see who made it. Then check out securely with Stripe and we ship it to you.

What's the QR code on my bag?

It leads to the artist and their work, so you can follow them and see more of what they make.

For artists and venues

I'm an artist. How do I get my work in a machine?

Fill out our artist application. Accepted artists earn 50% of every sale. Applications open throughout the year, so join our newsletter or follow @mystrymart to hear when the next one opens.

Do I need to live in New York?

Most of our artists are New York creatives, but we've featured artists from elsewhere too. Tell us where you're based when you apply.

Who paints the machines?

Artists do! When a new venue partners with us, we hold a design competition. Artists submit machine designs inspired by the location's vision and aesthetic, and the winning design is painted onto the machine. To hear when the next competition opens, follow @mystrymart on Instagram or join our newsletter at the bottom of this page.

Can I host a machine at my venue?

We'd love to hear from you. Galleries, museums, markets and shops can reach us through our contact page.
